# Battle backgrounds

The bundled plains and forest backgrounds and their matching ground platforms
are by WAve, from the [Red Sunset Graphics Set](https://feuniverse.us/t/fire-emblem-resource-repository-battle-animations-portraits-music-etc/3326/1420).
WAve released the set as free to use and edit and credited Feaw's red sunset
tileset as inspiration.

The PNGs are the original community uploads. Godot crops their viewport and
uses the first platform tile at runtime. To supply your own backdrops, see
[local overrides](../local_battle_backgrounds/README.md).
